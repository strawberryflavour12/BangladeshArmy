ABOUT US

Bangladesh Army

The Bangladesh Army is the land warfare branch of the Bangladesh Armed Forces.

Its primary responsibility is to defend the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Bangladesh.

The Army also contributes to national development, disaster management and humanitarian assistance.
